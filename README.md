## The 365-night trial and what returning actually looks like

Every Nectar mattress comes with a 365-night home trial and the Forever Warranty, and both have real teeth — with conditions worth knowing before you order.

The trial works like this: delivery starts the clock, and from day 30 through day 365 you can initiate a return by phone (1-888-425-4854) or email (returns@nectarsleep.com). Nectar arranges pickup or local donation of the mattress and refunds your original payment method in full. Returns are free within the contiguous 48 states. The 30-day minimum exists because new memory foam mattresses take two to four weeks to break in and often feel firmer at first — that's normal, and the yearlong window exists precisely so you don't judge it on night three.

Two caveats: free shipping and returns apply only to the contiguous US, so Alaska and Hawaii buyers are paying that $350 each way on a return, and the Forever Warranty — full replacement for defects in the first 10 years, repair thereafter — is non-transferable and covers manufacturing defects, not comfort preferences.
